# Chapter 9: Chart Design Principles & Storytelling

## 📌 Key Topics & Subtopics
- [ ] **Chart Selection Matrix**
  - Comparison: Bar chart, Column chart
  - Trend over time: Line chart, Area chart
  - Distribution: Histogram, Density plot, Box plot
  - Relationship: Scatter plot, Bubble chart
  - Composition: Stacked bar chart, Treemap
- [ ] **Visual Hierarchy & Color Theory**
  - Accent colors vs neutral backgrounds
  - Colorblind-friendly palettes (Viridis, Colorbrewer)
- [ ] **Data-to-Ink Ratio & Clutter Reduction**
  - Eliminating unnecessary gridlines, borders, and redundant legends

---
## 💡 Practice Exercise Idea
Audit 3 existing charts from news or business reports, identify design flaws, and redesign them on paper or slide mockups following design best practices.
